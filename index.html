account_balance About the Programme

Presidency University, Kolkata proposes to host a five-day Faculty Development Program (FDP) titled CryptoNext: Mathematical and Engineering Perspectives on Post-Quantum Security , scheduled for November 2–6, 2026. The programme is organized in joint collaboration with our Hub institute, the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata, under the Information Security Education and Awareness (ISEA) Project Phase-III, a flagship initiative of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

subject Read the complete programme description expand_more

The FDP is uniquely designed for faculty members across Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering, and allied STEM disciplines who are interested in the intersection of data security and cryptography.

The academic scope of the programme covers the fundamental principles of cryptography, cybersecurity, and cybercrime, advancing into the critical, modern domain of post-quantum cryptography. The sessions are uniquely structured to provide both rigorous mathematical foundations and practical engineering perspectives.

verified

Upon successful completion of the FDP, registered participants will receive a formal certificate from the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

Final candidate selections will be finalized and communicated to the respective participants via their registered email addresses.

campaign Important · Limited Seats
